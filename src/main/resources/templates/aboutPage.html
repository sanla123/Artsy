A LITTLE ABOUT US

Made for people who love making things.

Artsy started as a simple idea: creative people deserve a home that's actually built for them — not a general marketplace they have to squeeze into.

🧵

Our story

Many creative people — painters, crocheters, jewelry makers, journal decorators — struggle to find specialized supplies in one place, or a real audience for their handmade work.

Artsy was built to solve that: a single, organized marketplace for creative supplies, handmade goods, and digital creative assets, so creators and buyers don't have to piece things together across scattered shops and social media.

What we stand for
